CURRENCY MARKET UPDATE
During the week, the U.S. Dollar Index (a relative measure of the U.S. Dollar (USD) against a basket of
influential currencies) shed 0.5%, indicating a weakening of the U.S. Dollar. The Rwandan Franc
depreciated slightly against the U.S. Dollar by 3.0 bps to close at Frw 1,461.3, up from Frw 1,460.9,
recorded the previous week, taking the YTD depreciation to 0.6%. The Rwandan Franc depreciated
against the Euro and Pound by 0.7% and 0.8% to close at Frw 1,721.8 and Frw 1,976.4, up from their
respective rates of Frw 1,710.7 and Frw 1,960.9, recorded the previous week. The Rwandan Franc
appreciated against all other currencies apart from the Kenyan shilling as shown below.
